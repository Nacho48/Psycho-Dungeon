What the app is

Psycho Dungeon gameplay is a small desktop tool that runs on the owner's own Windows PC. It takes gameplay clips of the video game Psycho Dungeon (also known as Mazmorra en Directo), edits them into vertical videos, and uploads them to the user's own TikTok account as drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API. The user reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

Who can use it

The tool is operated by the Psycho Dungeon developer for the game's own TikTok account. It is not offered as a service to the public. Anyone who uses it must have a TikTok account in good standing and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

What the tool does on your account

  • It signs you in with TikTok Login Kit and asks for two permissions: user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • It only uploads videos to your drafts inbox. It never publishes anything, never deletes content, and never reads your messages or followers.
  • You can stop it at any time by revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions).

Your content

You keep all rights to your gameplay videos. You are responsible for what you choose to publish, including any music or sound you add in the TikTok editor.
