Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

  1. Who is responsible

    The developer of Psycho Dungeon is responsible for the data described here. Contact: https://github.com/Nacho48/Psycho-Dungeon/issues.

  2. No data from other people

    Psycho Dungeon gameplay is a private tool that only we use, with our own TikTok account. It does not collect, receive or store personal data from any other person: not from viewers, not from followers, not from anyone who visits this website.

  3. What the tool handles, and only for our own account

    • Our TikTok sign-in: the access token, refresh token and account ID (open_id) that TikTok returns when we connect our account. They are kept only on our own computer and are used only to upload drafts.
    • Our gameplay videos: clips of the game recorded on our computer, edited and sent to our TikTok drafts.
    • A local log of which videos were uploaded and when.

    None of this is sent to any server of ours (we have none), sold, or shared with anyone except TikTok, which receives the videos we upload.

  4. The game is fiction

    Psycho Dungeon is a work of fiction. The "reality show", its broadcasts, audience, sponsors, brands, contestants and the AI that runs the dungeon are all invented game elements. There are no real sponsors, no real contestants and no real show. Any resemblance to real people, companies or programmes is coincidental. No real person's data is used to create them.

  5. This website

    This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

  6. Keeping and deleting

    The TikTok tokens are kept while the tool is connected and are deleted when we disconnect it. We can revoke the tool's access at any time from the TikTok app.

  7. Your rights

    If you believe we hold any data about you, you can ask us to access or delete it through the contact above. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

  8. Changes

    We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
