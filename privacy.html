Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Short version

Psycho Dungeon gameplay runs only on your own PC. It has no servers, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not receive, store or share any of your data.

Data the tool handles

  • TikTok sign-in data: when you connect your account, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token and your open_id. They are saved only on your PC, in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\MazmorraTikTok\), and are used only to upload drafts to your account.
  • Your videos: gameplay clips recorded on your PC and the edited videos made from them. They are sent only to TikTok, to your drafts inbox, when the tool uploads them.
  • A local log of which videos were uploaded and when, stored on your PC.

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic: needed by TikTok Login Kit to identify your account.
  • video.upload: to send videos to your drafts inbox. The tool never publishes directly.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone except TikTok itself, which receives the videos you upload. TikTok's handling of that content is covered by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

  • Revoke access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.
  • Delete the folder %APPDATA%\MazmorraTikTok\ on your PC to remove the saved tokens.
  • Delete drafts from your TikTok inbox as usual.

Children

The tool is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok.
