Psycho Dungeon

A pixel-art roguelike set inside a fictional reality show. An invented AI runs the dungeon, made-up sponsors pay for the show and the other contestants decide whether to help you or sell you out.

Psycho Dungeon is a work of fiction. The "reality show", its broadcasts, audience, sponsors, brands, contestants and the AI that runs the dungeon are all invented game elements. There are no real sponsors, no real contestants and no real show. Any resemblance to real people, companies or programmes is coincidental.
